📚 Frequently Asked Questions

Can you recover my account directly?

No. We provide general guidance only. Use the platform's official recovery methods.

Should I share my password or OTP?

No. Never share passwords, OTPs, verification codes or recovery codes.

What should I do if I cannot log in?

Use the official account recovery option provided by the platform and follow its instructions.

How can I report impersonation?

Use the platform's official impersonation reporting tool and submit truthful information.

Who can submit a copyright report?

Copyright reports should generally be submitted by the copyright owner or an authorized representative.

How can I make my account safer?

Use a strong unique password, enable two-factor authentication when available and review login activity.

Do you guarantee account recovery?

No. Final decisions about account recovery are made by the relevant platform.
